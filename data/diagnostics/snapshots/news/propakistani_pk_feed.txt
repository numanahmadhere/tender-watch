										<content:encoded><![CDATA[<p>Pakistan’s National Cyber Emergency Response Team (National CERT) has directed government organizations to preserve digital evidence during cybersecurity investigations, warning that changes to system records could compromise forensic analysis of cyber incidents.</p>
<p>Under the National Cybersecurity Handbook 2026–27, government entities are required to cooperate with National CERT-led investigations by providing timely physical and administrative access to affected infrastructure and relevant technical records.</p>

<p>The guidelines state that detailed investigations and digital forensics are essential to determine the causes of security breaches, assess their impact and help contain cyber threats.</p>
<h3>Don&#8217;t Alter System Logs</h3>
<p>The handbook instructs government personnel to maintain a proper chain of custody for affected devices and storage media so that digital evidence remains intact and traceable throughout an investigation.</p>
<p>Employees have been advised not to modify or overwrite system audit logs, firewall records or disk memory dumps before investigation teams collect them. Such changes could undermine efforts to reconstruct incidents and establish how a breach occurred.</p>
<p>Relevant personnel must also provide investigators with operational details, network diagrams and timelines of the incident to support the forensic process.</p>
<h3>Unauthorized Access Restricted</h3>
<p>National CERT has cautioned government entities against allowing unverified third-party vendors or unauthorized personnel to interfere with systems isolated during an active investigation.</p>
<p>Organizations have also been instructed to implement emergency containment measures and remediation guidance issued by National CERT investigation teams.</p>
<p>According to the handbook, affected services must not be restored and disconnected network segments must not be reconnected until formal security clearance has been granted. The requirement is intended to prevent systems from returning to operation before investigators and security teams have addressed the incident.</p>
<h3>Cyber Incidents Must Be Reported</h3>
<p>The handbook also refers to the CERT Rules 2023, under which cybersecurity incidents must be reported through approved channels, including organizational, provincial or sectoral CERTs.</p>

<p>The reporting and evidence-preservation requirements form part of the procedures intended to help authorities investigate cyber incidents, identify vulnerabilities and coordinate responses across government organizations.</p>
<p>The guidance places responsibility on government entities to protect relevant records, provide investigators with the information they need and follow containment and recovery instructions until clearance is issued.</p>

										<content:encoded><![CDATA[<p>A free and open-source app called DigUp lets users search files on their computer by describing what they contain instead of relying only on filenames or exact keywords.</p>
<p>Available for Mac, Windows, and Linux, DigUp uses Google DeepMind’s EmbeddingGemma 2 model to search photos, PDFs, documents, audio, videos, and, optionally, source code.</p>
<p>Users can search phrases such as “zebra in a video,” “where they talk about sleep,” or “the clause about pets in the lease.” DigUp can then open the matching video or audio at the relevant moment or take users directly to the correct PDF page.</p>

<h2>Powered by Google DeepMind’s EmbeddingGemma 2</h2>
<p>Google DeepMind released EmbeddingGemma 2 in October 2026.</p>
<p>The 740-million-parameter model places text, images, audio, and video into the same shared space, allowing a text query to match content inside different file types.</p>
<p>It supports more than 100 languages and is available under the Apache 2.0 license. Cross-language searches also work, meaning an English query can find content written in Bengali, Arabic, or other supported languages.</p>
<p>DigUp also combines semantic search with exact matching for things such as names, invoice numbers and error codes.</p>
<p><a href="https://propakistani.pk/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/demo.gif"><img fetchpriority="high" decoding="async" class=" wp-image-1103048 aligncenter" src="https://propakistani.pk/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/demo.gif" alt="" width="801" height="534" /></a></p>
<h2>Everything Runs Locally</h2>
<p>DigUp says indexed files never leave the user’s computer.</p>
<p>The app only connects to the internet to download its 865MB model once and to check for updates once per day. The update check can be disabled.</p>
<p>There is no account requirement and no telemetry.</p>
<p>Users choose which folders DigUp can access, and it does not change, move, or upload files.</p>

<h2>What DigUp Can Search</h2>
<p>DigUp supports common image formats, PDFs including scans, documents such as TXT, Markdown, RTF, DOCX, ODT and HTML, plus audio and video of any length.</p>
<p>Audio is processed in overlapping 30-second sections, while videos are indexed using sampled frames and their soundtracks.</p>
<p>Optional code search can find functions by describing what they do. Results can open directly at the relevant line in editors including VS Code, Cursor, Zed, Sublime Text, and JetBrains IDEs.</p>

										<content:encoded><![CDATA[<p>The Strategic Vision Institute (SVI), Islamabad, hosts the second iteration of the Islamabad Non-Proliferation Conference 2026, a two-day international event bringing together a distinguished group of scholars and experts from around the world.</p>
<p>The conference aims to foster constructive dialogue on global strategic issues and share Pakistan’s policy perspectives.</p>
<p>Dr Naeem Salik, Executive Director (ED), SVI, welcomed the distinguished guests to the International Nonproliferation Conference (INC) 2026. Dr Nasir Hafeez, Director, SVI, subsequently introduced the conference to the esteemed audience.</p>
<p>The inaugural address was delivered by Lt. Gen. Sarfraz Sattar, HI (M), (Retd.), Advisor, NCA. He mentioned that the disruptive technologies sit at the heart of the push towards bigger and better Nuclear Weapons, against the efforts of restraint or stability on the road to disarmament and sustained economic development.</p>

<p>He said that there has been a shift in the nuclear age marked by the change of threat perception from rogue or non-state actors getting access to nuclear weapons towards the challenge of Great Power Competition and the possibility of interstate conflict in a more contested international system.</p>
<p>He mentioned South Asia as a nuclear flashpoint, owing to India’s ultranationalist tendencies. We face Hindutva ideology, which is in pursuit of Akhand Bharat.</p>
<p>He further mentioned that the nuclear non-use taboo seems to be weakening as we see the likelihood of interstate wars increasing both in lethality and expense. The sole function of the global nuclear order is also under extreme stress.</p>
<p>Lt. Gen. Khalid Ahmed Kidwai, NI, HI, HI (M), (Retd.), Advisor Development, National Command Authority (NCA), delivered the keynote address. He examined Pakistan’s perspective on credible deterrence, strategic balance, and strategic stability in South Asia, tracing their evolution in the pre-May 2025 conflict period and assessing their implications in the post-conflict strategic environment.</p>
<p>Lt Gen. Kidwai (Retd.) emphasized that Pakistan’s Full Spectrum Deterrence (FSD) is fundamentally defensive, aimed at preventing aggression across the entire spectrum of conflict and preserving strategic stability in South Asia.</p>
<p><img decoding="async" class=" wp-image-1103035 aligncenter" src="https://propakistani.pk/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/WhatsApp-Image-2026-10-20-at-5.47.49-PM.jpeg" alt="" width="575" height="863" srcset="https://propakistani.pk/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/WhatsApp-Image-2026-10-20-at-5.47.49-PM.jpeg 1066w, https://propakistani.pk/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/WhatsApp-Image-2026-10-20-at-5.47.49-PM-1023x1536.jpeg 1023w, https://propakistani.pk/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/WhatsApp-Image-2026-10-20-at-5.47.49-PM.avif 1066w, https://propakistani.pk/wp-content/uploads/2026/10/WhatsApp-Image-2026-10-20-at-5.47.49-PM-1023x1536.avif 1023w" sizes="(max-width: 575px) 100vw, 575px" /></p>
<p>Pakistan’s Full Spectrum Deterrence generated strategic effects in both regional and international arenas in the May 2025 India-Pakistan conflict, as the role of conventional and nuclear deterrence in limiting escalation was demonstrated.</p>
<p>He highlighted SIPRI Yearbook 2026 findings on India’s deployment of approximately 12 nuclear warheads aboard a nuclear submarine for deterrence patrols, expressing concern that the absence of pre-notification of Indian SLBM tests could undermine South Asian strategic stability, raise questions about India’s NFU policy, and increase the risks of miscalculation and nuclear escalation.</p>
<p>He reaffirmed Pakistan’s right to take appropriate measures to safeguard its national security and preserve the regional strategic balance. Furthermore, he stressed the importance of preventing technological imbalances from undermining strategic stability in South Asia.</p>

<p>To comprehensively address the spectrum of challenges, the conference hosts seven working sessions on critical topics such as deterrence dynamics and escalation risks in South Asia, the evolving deterrence landscape in a transforming global nuclear order, and the collapse of the arms control architecture and challenges to the non-proliferation regime.</p>
<p>Each session facilitates in-depth discussions, aiming at generating policy-relevant recommendations in both national and global security frameworks. The conference will be concluded with an address by Gen. Zubair Mahmood Hayat, NI (M), (Retd.), Former Chairman, Joint Chiefs of Staff Committee (CJCSC).</p>

<p>The move follows concerns raised by the higher judiciary over the misuse of Section 406 of the PPC, which deals with criminal breach of trust.</p>
<p>Courts have observed that many cases registered under Section 406 do not meet the legal requirements for criminal breach of trust, resulting in individuals being jailed for offenses that are not established at the initial stage.</p>
<p>Following these observations, the Lahore police&#8217;s investigation wing has canceled more than 200 cases registered under Section 406 across the city and issued new Standard Operating Procedures (SOPs) for investigating similar complaints.</p>

<p>A senior police official said most fraud and cheating complaints should be handled under Section 420. However, police have frequently registered cases under the non-bailable Section 406, particularly in financial and property disputes.</p>
<p>The official said the proposed amendment would allow authorities to take stricter action against fraud suspects while addressing concerns over the misuse of Section 406.</p>
<p>The proposal was discussed during a meeting chaired by Lahore Capital City Police Officer (CCPO) Bilal Siddique Kamyana, attended by senior investigation officials.</p>
